How it Works

  1. Choose your farm. The map opens at your current location if your browser allows it. Tap the map to pin a point, or choose Draw farm boundary and tap each corner of your field. You can also search for a place or type coordinates.
  2. Pick an analysis. Historical Trends shows what really happened, measured by NASA satellites and models. Future Scenarios shows what climate models expect.
  3. Choose what to see, such as rainfall or temperature, and the time period.
  4. Read the chart. The summary above the chart gives the average, the highest and lowest values, and whether things are getting wetter, drier, warmer or cooler.

NASA data covers areas of about 10–50 km, so a small farm shows the conditions of its surrounding area.

About

AgriClimate Explorer helps farmers see the climate of their own land using free NASA Earth data.

  • Historical data: NASA Giovanni (through the NASA Harmony API): GPM IMERG rainfall, GLDAS soil moisture, temperature and evapotranspiration, and MERRA-2 daily temperatures.
  • Future data: NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 climate projections, averaged over three climate models.
