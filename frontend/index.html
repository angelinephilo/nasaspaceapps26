How it Works

  1. Select Region: Search a place/coordinates, click a point on the map, or draw your farm boundary polygon.
  2. Choose Analysis: Pick Historical Trends (past satellite data) or Future Scenarios (CMIP6 climate projections).
  3. Configure & View: Select your crop-relevant variable and timeframe to generate a tailored chart.

About This Tool

Powered by NASA Earthdata (Giovanni / Harmony API) and Google Maps, helping farmers assess historical weather patterns and future climate risks for their fields.
